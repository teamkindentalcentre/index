Paste your Google Apps Script Web App URL. Every trade saves automatically to your Sheet.

📋 Google Sheets Setup (5 minutes)

STEP 1 — Open Google Sheets, create a new spreadsheet named "Options Tracker".


STEP 2 — Click Extensions → Apps Script.


STEP 3 — Delete all existing code, paste the google_apps_script.js file contents, save.


STEP 4 — Click Deploy → New Deployment. Type = Web App, Access = Anyone. Copy the URL.


STEP 5 — Paste URL above, click Test Connection.


STEP 6 — Upload this HTML to Google Drive. Open in Chrome from any device.


⚠️ Google may ask to authorise the script — click Allow.
